# Personalise your Lifequest — the prompt

Copy everything inside the box into a new Claude conversation where the **lifequest** skill is turned on. Fill in what you already know; leave the rest blank. Claude asks for what's missing, in short rounds.

No skill installed? Attach the files from the `lifequest` folder to the conversation instead; the prompt works the same way.

## Tips

- **Start small.** Turn on one module at most for the first two weeks. Add XP or KPIs once the weekly rhythm sticks.
- **Journal lightly.** Three lines a day are enough. The planning is only as good as what it can read.
- **Change the config any time.** Tell Claude "update my Lifequest config: …" or edit `lifequest_config.md` yourself.
- **Your data stays yours.** The config and journals are plain files in your own folder. Don't put anything in them you wouldn't want an AI to read.
