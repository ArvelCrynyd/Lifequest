# Quest design

## Anatomy of a quest

| Part | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Name | thematic, in the config's genre, 2–5 words | *The Path of Ten Minutes* |
| Full | the real task, concrete, with a size (minutes, count, place) | 10-minute walk, no phone, no podcast |
| Flex | the smallest version that still counts, ≤ 2 minutes or one action | 5 minutes, phone in the pocket |
| Window | from the category's time window in the config | after work |

A quest is done when **either** Full or Flex is done. Flex is not failure; it is the system working.

## Sizing

- Default Full size: 5–15 minutes. Movement may go to 30. Anything longer becomes a main-quest subquest.
- Flex: one action you could do while tired, ill or travelling.
- Weekly quests may be larger (30–60 min) because the player picks the day.
- Respect `daily_quest_ceiling`. If a day is crowded with events, drop to Flex-only quests and say so.

## Naming

- Name the *feeling* or the *place*, not the chore: *Potion Prep* (clean one mug, pan or bottle), *Realm Restore* (reset the desk for the new week).
- Reuse names for recurring rituals so they become familiar ("Morning Ritual — The Quiet Brew"); vary the second half.
- Match `tone.flavour`: `light` = plain title plus one themed word; `full` = fully in-world names and a one-line flavour text.
- Never let the name hide the task. The Full line always says plainly what to do.

## Context rules

| Situation | Adjust |
|---|---|
| travel day, long appointment | micro-quests only, Movement can be "walk to the station" |
| low-energy pattern on a weekday | Flex-first wording, move effortful quests to better days |
| a category slipped 3+ weeks | change the *kind* of quest or its time, not just the count |
| big deadline or event | the main quest supports it; daily quests stay small |
| free weekend | one ambitious Full version, the rest normal |

## Main quest

One per week. 2–3 subquests, each producing something you can point to (a draft, a cleared shelf, a sent message). Full and Flex for each subquest, like daily quests.

## Journal questions (for Journaling quests)

What was the highlight of my day? · What surprised me today? · What's one small win? · What energised me, and what drained me? · Who made my day better, and why? · What could I have done differently? · What's one moment I want to remember? · Did I step outside my comfort zone? · What am I looking forward to tomorrow?

## Writing prompts (for Creative quests)

"An invitation with no sender." · "A sealed room without a door." · "The map leads where it shouldn't." · "An echo answers before you speak." · "Who wrote the footnote?" · "A light from the wrong direction." · "A book with pages added overnight." · "An object that remembers more than its owner." · "The mirror reflects someone else." · "A letter arrives, postmarked 50 years in the future." · "Someone rewrites your diary." · "The ritual was going well — until the candles blinked out."
