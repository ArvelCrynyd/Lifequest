# Module: Flagship project

One main project per season (a novel, a game, a course, a renovation). The flagship gets steady, small weekly deliverables instead of heroic weekends.

## Inputs

- `flagship.project` — the name.
- `flagship.roadmap_file` — a roadmap in `planning/` (milestones, what "done" means). If it's missing, ask the player in the review whether to draft one together.
- `flagship.hours_per_week` — the honest budget (default 4).

## Each week

1. Read the roadmap and last week's progress from the journal.
2. Pick **1–3 "must ship" deliverables** that fit the budget. A deliverable is an artefact that exists afterwards — a page, a draft, a sketch, a test build — never "work on X".
3. Spread the time into the Creative (or project) category as blocks, e.g. 2 × 90 min draft, 1 × 60 min revise, 1 × 30 min planning. Flex versions are 10–20 minutes on the same deliverable.
4. If the week is crowded, cut the deliverable, not the Flex.
5. One flagship deliverable may be this week's main quest.

## Guardrails

- Only one flagship at a time; a second project can run, but without a weekly deliverable.
- When a busy month is announced in the config or events (a writing challenge, a move, exams), halve the budget for that month.
- Scope creep: if the roadmap grows three weeks in a row, say so and suggest cutting.
